From data extraction tool to end-to-end analytics

Supermetrics helps marketers collect and analyse data from hundreds of platforms. As the company evolved from a data extraction tool into an end-to-end analytics product, the Transformation space was created to let users clean, combine and model data without code.

I led end-to-end design for the Transformation product space, defining strategy and vision together with the PM, and collaborating with engineering and solutions experts on four features shipped to production.

Blending data, without the jargon

Templates

Blending data is one of the most powerful yet intimidating parts of analytics. Performance marketers didn’t know what a blend was, or why they’d need one.

I designed a template library organised around real use cases, framed around the value and outcome for the user, such as paid campaign mix or cross-channel ROAS. Each template guides users step by step through the blend, keeping focus on the outcome.

3×Users blended data up to 3× faster, with consistent adoption across teams of different technical skill.
+224%Year-on-year growth in data searches using Data Blending, alongside the blending templates we shipped. Source: Supermetrics Wrapped 2025.
Joins, the hardest kind of blend

Cross-platform analysis depends on joins, yet most marketers neither know what a join is nor why they need it.

I designed a guided flow that translates SQL logic into a visual process: select sources, define join logic in plain language, and instantly preview results.

Custom fields in context

Marketers often needed to adjust data on the fly, but creating calculated fields meant complex formulas and leaving the workflow.

I designed a no-code interface for defining custom fields directly in context, with instant previews that show the impact in real time.

+125%Year-on-year growth in data searches using Custom Fields. Marketers are building their own metrics. Source: Supermetrics Wrapped 2025.

Account tagging

Large marketing teams manage dozens of ad accounts across platforms. My goal was to design a system that lets users organise accounts the way that suits them and reuse those groups in reports, which removes repetitive setup and manual errors.

Connect any source

Supermetrics has 100+ native connectors, but large customers often needed a source we didn’t support, like a private API. Connector Builder lets teams build that connector themselves, low-code, starting from a template and with an AI assistant to help.

Version 1Foundations firstA section for each part of a connector, with a template to start from. We opened it to a small group of partners to test before releasing it more widely.
Version 2Then AIOnce partners were using the sections and templates successfully, we added AI. It reads the API docs, drafts the connector and suggests the right templates.

A custom connector is shared with the whole team and behaves like a native one, so a missing source no longer blocks a deal or a dashboard.

Making technical tools approachable can mean designing clear structures and language around them, without taking away what they can do.

ReflectionMy biggest challenge was the technical constraints of legacy code. Much of the work was negotiation: showing engineering and product that extra effort on the code underneath could unlock a far better experience, and that a better experience is what drives growth.
